# Demo Script (5–7 Minutes)

1. **Public Homepage**
   - Highlight the hero section and enterprise tone.
   - Show the service list and partner logo carousel placeholders.

2. **Service Detail**
   - Navigate to any service detail (e.g., Full Maintenance Lease).
   - Point out benefits, process steps, and the Request a Quote CTA.

3. **Publications + Events**
   - Open Publications and Company Events to show content richness.

4. **Contact Form to CRM**
   - Submit a Request a Quote via Contact Us.

5. **Intranet Login**
   - Sign in with demo credentials (admin@njiloconsulting.co.za / admin123).

6. **CRM Leads**
   - Show the newly submitted lead in the Leads module.

7. **Operations + HR**
   - Visit Vehicles, Work Orders, and Vacancies to show operations coverage.

8. **Content Management**
   - Open Partner Logos and Video Gallery to show CMS controls.

9. **Admin**
   - Highlight User Management and Audit Log for governance readiness.
